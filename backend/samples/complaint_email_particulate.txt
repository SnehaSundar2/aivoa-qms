Subject: URGENT - Particles found in Ondansetron vials, batch OND25B119

Dear Quality Assurance team,

I am writing to formally raise a complaint on behalf of Lakeside Regional
Medical Center regarding Ondansetron Injection USP 2 mg/mL, 2 mL single-dose
vials, batch OND25B119, expiry 05/2028.

During routine pre-administration inspection this morning, our oncology nursing
staff identified small floating particles in four vials from a carton of 25.
The particles are translucent and fibre-like and are clearly visible when the
vial is held against a black background under the inspection lamp. One of our
pharmacists independently confirmed the observation.

No product from this carton has been administered to any patient. All 25 vials
from the affected carton have been withdrawn from the ward and quarantined in
our pharmacy under lock. The four affected vials are being held for return and
we can ship them to you on request.

We received 200 vials of this batch on 12 August 2026 under purchase order
LRM-PO-88214. The remaining stock is being held pending your response.

Given that this is an injectable product administered to oncology patients,
we consider this extremely serious and request a response within 24 hours,
including your assessment of whether other batches are affected.
